Abstract

Vision-Language Models (VLMs) excel at open-world 2D perception but struggle with precise metric spatial reasoning, a key requirement for applications like autonomous driving, navigation, and embodied intelligence. We identify this as a structural limitation: images are 2D projections of the 3D world, inherently suffering from projective ambiguity, while VLM components favor semantic understanding and rely on 2D positional bias, leading to Spatial Collapse. To address this, we propose Despa, a depth-grounded spatial VLM that injects depth-based geometric information via Geometric Positional Embedding (GPE) and Depth-aware Rotational Positional Embedding (DoPE). To expose masked limitations in current benchmarks, we construct SpaDataset (1.7M QA pairs) and SpaBench (2,051 QAs), to our knowledge the largest fine-grained training corpus and benchmark suite for metric spatial reasoning. They cover three difficulty levels and ten sub-tasks across diverse settings. The fine-tuned Despa-4B model outperforms Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B and GPT-5.2 on SpaBench by 27.9 and 31.1 percentage points, respectively. With separate fine-tuning on each corresponding training dataset, Despa-4B reaches 66.3% on MSMU, 42.4% on RefSpatial-U, and 66.0% on the multi-frame VSI-Bench. Despa consistently improves LLaVA-1.5, Qwen2.5-VL, and Qwen3-VL backbones, achieving strong metric spatial reasoning with only a 4B model.

Spatial Collapse

Nearby in 2D can be far apart in 3D. An image flattens the world. Today's VLMs inherit that geometry: semantic tokens compress metric depth, while positional encodings treat patches close in the image as neighbors—even across a large depth gap.

Token-side collapse

Vision tokens preserve what an object is, while compressing its exact depth and physical scale.

Position-side collapse

Multimodal RoPE encodes time, height and width. Depth separation has no explicit positional axis.

Method

Despa restores geometry at two interfaces: GPE adds explicit geometric anchors to visual tokens, and DoPE introduces a depth axis to attention while preserving most of the pretrained positional structure.

Token features

GPE Ground the tokens.

Geometric Positional Embedding adds a sinusoidal metric anchor to every visual token: patch depth for images, shared world coordinates for videos.

Experiments

We evaluate Despa across spatial benchmarks, component ablations, and VLM backbones. Despa-4B reaches 61.8% on SpaBench, improving over same-data Direct SFT by 5.7 percentage points.
